# Predicting Innovation Impact in Biomedical Research

Analysed **27,226 PubMed records** to identify what separates highly-cited research from quickly-forgotten work, testing theories of recombinant novelty, optimal distinctiveness, and network structure.

**Tools:** R · **Methods:** random forest, decision trees, SVM, LDA, LASSO · **Outcome:** classification of high-citation research and its drivers
