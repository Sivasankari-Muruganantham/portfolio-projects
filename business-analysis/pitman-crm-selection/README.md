# Pitman Training — CRM Selection & Website Re-Design

A full business analysis engagement (consultancy framing: Caldwell Foster Advisory) for Pitman Training — a training provider managing 27,000+ contacts in spreadsheets with no CRM.

**My role:** Business Analyst — problem definition through project closure.

## Deliverables
1. **Business Case** — evaluation of Salesforce vs ActiveCampaign vs MailChimp against a £97k budget, 10 users, and a 3-month implementation window, with a formal recommendation to senior stakeholders.
2. **AS-IS Process Map** (draw.io) — swimlane model of the current sales process exposing manual handoffs and lost leads.
3. **TO-BE Use Case Diagram** — target operating model for ActiveCampaign across email marketing, lead management, and reporting.
4. **Requirements Catalogue** — epics and user stories with MoSCoW prioritisation and testable acceptance criteria.
5. **Balsamiq Wireframes** — redesigned course page and 3-step call-booking journey.
6. **End-of-Project Report** — performance against objectives, lessons learned, follow-on actions.

**Skills:** business case development · options appraisal · process modelling · requirements engineering · wireframing · stakeholder communication
